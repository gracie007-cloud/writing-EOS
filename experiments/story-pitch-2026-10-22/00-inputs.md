## Story Braindump (material)

From the Character Pool cards and the earlier worked example (Skin regenerated for this book's world):

- **Hari Harlan** (CHAR-407), 47, Chief Officer of **The Lightyear** (CHAR-300), the grand way-station of the routes. Even-tempered, scrupulously fair: fleets and their deserters dock at the same rate.
- First office: keeper of the **Shear Crossing**, a gravity-tide belt between the Near and Far Moorings. In all her years the crossing lost no one. Ghost: the **Shear Storm**.
- A cosmic **phenomenon** bonded her telepathically to **Lyra** (CHAR-313), the station's seventh mind. The two keep the station running between them. What the phenomenon is stays **settled-open**: never explained.
- Want: the station kept running, every ship docked kindly, every soul across.
- Lie: "If I hold the line myself, no one is lost."
- Need: let others keep the station, and learn which of her thoughts are hers.
- Deputy question unfilled; the worked example cast **Jonick Renrick** (CHAR-294), the Pathfinder's redeemed double-post chief (engineer and helmsman).
- Settings in reach: **Nova Lumen** (CHAR-304, E-class stage-world of courts and fleets), **Nirisha Nova** (CHAR-305, hollowed meteor in its orbit: night market, bohemian quarter, conscience), **Lunara** (CHAR-443, moon resort, "the Serene").

## The Author's pitch house style

The Story Pitches pool writes premises in one recognisable form: *What if…? / This is not X. This is Y. / Every… Every… / There are no X here. Only…* Both versions use it for the premise paragraph.
