## The rule

- **Canon**: a human value, condition or question a character's Lie-to-Truth can turn on, and that a book's argument can be built around. A character's arc carries a Canon theme.
- **Motif**: a mode, texture, setting or world element, or subject area that recurs and colours the book without being what a character learns. Motifs support a Canon theme; they don't replace it.
