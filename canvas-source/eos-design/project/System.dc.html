The rule

Writer view (default) shows the story, the work in plain words and your decisions. Engine view adds what the machine is doing: the agent's real name, its model slot and pool, budgets, cost, guards and the exact prompt.

Plain names come first everywhere ("Pick the characters"); the agent names from your plans appear in the Engine view.
