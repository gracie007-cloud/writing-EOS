Your final call, at every level

Agents never settle these. They pitch: a recommendation, the options they weighed, and why. You proceed, choose another, or give your own alternative.
  • The pen name and the adapted premise
  • The Conflict Core pick, the Plot Blueprint, the arc’s outcome and the structure
  • Characters and names for Tiers 1 and 2
  • The representation and trope decision
  • Locking the Bible and every change to canon
  • Locking a chapter
  • Mature-tier routing and its endpoints
  • Scoring drafts, model assignments and prompt edits
  • Escalations, and anything over the cost cap
An agent may decide only when
  1. its confidence clears the section’s bar,
  2. a checker from a different model family agrees, and
  3. it has earned the level in the Model Lab by agreeing with your own past choices.
You can set a level that hasn’t been earned; those decisions are marked in your digest.
Back to Supervised automatically when
  • you undo 2 of a section’s last 10 decisions,
  • a later check contradicts a decision it made, or
  • 25 decisions pile up without you opening the digest.
A batch also stops at its cost cap, whatever the level.
